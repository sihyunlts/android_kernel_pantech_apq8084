Pantech EF67 FC8080 terrestrial DMB
=================================

The EF67 DMB receiver uses BLSP1 QUP1 SPI, mode 0, 8-bit words,
10.8MHz maximum bus speed, GPIO 75 for a falling-edge interrupt and
GPIO 13 for chip enable. The S0226114 stock clock lookup is
clk_get_sys("dmb_clk", "xo"), backed by the RPM RF_CLK1 buffer.

Power sequence: chip enable high, 5ms delay, XO prepare/enable,
30ms delay. Power off: chip enable low, 1ms delay, XO disable/unprepare.
No regulator voltage is programmed by this driver. Probe leaves power off.

/dev/tdmb preserves the stock ioctl encodings ('t', commands 0 through 28).
The encoded size is one byte for many commands, but the stock payloads
are 4-byte scalars or larger structures. Do not change those ioctl numbers.
ARM payload sizes are: frequency 12, scan 12, channel 28, global-variable
12, FIC 388, signal quality 20 and stream packet 12684 bytes. A successful
read copies the stream packet and returns zero, as stock does. Stock
libdmb_service.so DMBTuner::BB_ReadMSC requests 15756 bytes. This count is
the userspace buffer capacity, not the exact kernel packet size: cap the
copy at 12684 bytes instead of rejecting the valid larger buffer.

The stream packet includes type, FIC size/data and MSC size/data. Unlike
the MSM8974 reference, EF67's read_msc receives the whole packet address.
Scan interrupts retain FIC for READ_FIB. Playback packets wake blocking
readers and generate SIGIO. Buffered packets are consumed once; shutdown
and removal wake readers. IRQ, control and data access share one mutex.

The FCI chip library and Korean channel/factory tables originate from
VegaDevTeam/android_kernel_pantech_msm8974, revision
76c11b449f0fa6cfe035c0bd0334041a378635fa, drivers/dmb. Original attribution
is retained. Only FC8080, SPI and 19.2MHz paths are included.

EF67-specific board and stream behavior follows the S0226114 kernel.
Initialization (63 register/delay operations), channel select/deselect,
tuner initialization and all 21 Korean frequencies match stock function
traces. Ioctl numbers/payload sizes and complete FIC/MSC packet contents
were compared with the stock binary, including FIFO wrap/full handling.

Safety differences: failed SPI operations cannot report successful
initialization; transfers use complete zero-padded DMA buffers; interrupt
lengths and user copies are bounded; a second open cannot power off the
first client; reader/IRQ shutdown is serialized. The stock chip-probe
retry sequence remains, with failure reported after all ten retries.
Unused EBI/TSIF/I2C host drivers, boot tests, dumps and timing logs are
excluded. The legacy EBI command copies through a checked userspace
buffer instead of treating its payload as a kernel pointer.

GCC 4.7 ARM kernel (zImage) and EF67 device tree compilation passed.
SPI transfer boundary and error-injection checks passed; extracted power
lifecycle checks cover clock/init failures and repeated shutdown.
On IM-A930S, station listing, IRQ activity and valid signal measurements
were observed. Playback failed with EINVAL because the initial read count
check rejected the stock library request. Native library call emulation
reproduces that failure and verifies the corrected capacity handling,
including FIC/MSC payload and untouched bytes beyond the kernel packet.
The user confirmed operation after the read-capacity correction.
Repeated start/stop and controlled reception comparisons remain to test.

Final review repeated chip/register/delay comparison across 126 cases
and signal-quality/antenna-bar calculations across 57 inputs against
S0226114. Channel restart clears cached packets and receive errors;
failed restart wakes waiting readers. Factory NETBER ignores uninitialized
or empty samples to prevent division by zero, preserving valid results.
Final GCC 4.7 ARM DMB driver compilation passed.
