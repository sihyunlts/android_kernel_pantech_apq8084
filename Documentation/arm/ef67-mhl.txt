EF67 SiI8620 MHL
===============

CONFIG_SII8620_MHL_TX enables the SPI-connected SiI8620 on EF67. It requires
the MDSS HDMI panel, MSM DWC3, SPI, power supplies and HID/input support.
The device is described by qcom,mhl-sii8620 under BLSP1 QUP2 (f9924000).

The common transmitter, EDID, HDCP, CBUS, RCP and MHL3 HID code comes from
Silicon Image's 2013-2014 GPL driver in the EF71 reference mhl3 directory.
Its starter-kit platform and userspace register/GPIO/BIST test interface
are not used. platform.c supplies the EF67 wiring instead.

The S0226114 kernel binary and DT provide the board-specific reference:
GPIO 48 interrupt, 35 chip power, 27 reset, 37/38 USB/MHL selection,
31 switch power and 94 firmware wake. The stock driver programs PMA8084
L14 to 1.85 V even though its original DT voltage property says 1.8 V.
The local DT expresses the voltage actually programmed by that driver.
The reference crystal is 24 MHz; SPI uses mode 0 at a maximum of 1 MHz.

USB ID detection hands the connector to MHL only after a 1 kohm cable
measurement. Discovery waits up to 500 ms (stock: 50 ticks at HZ=100).
Failure or disconnection stops the transmitter and charging, calls the
USB release callback, then lowers both switch selection pins. Queued
timer callbacks cannot access a powered-off transmitter; pending timer
work is drained before the next discovery. Initial standby and disconnect
work share the connection mutex. IRQ wake enable/disable is tracked across
disconnection during suspend. The DWC3 client
handoff preserves an online/offline callback received during discovery.

The HDMI source receives upstream HPD only after the transmitter's EDID
FIFO is prepared. Disconnect clears HPD. Its maximum pixel clock is
297000 kHz, as in the stock platform driver. HDMI registration is undone
if probing fails so a deferred probe does not leave a stale downstream
registration behind.

The mhl power supply reports PRESENT, ONLINE and CURRENT_MAX to battery.
The EF67 SMB135x current selection uses it while MHL owns the connector.
Discovery begins at 500 mA and sink capabilities select 100/500/900/1500
mA; the existing battery and temperature limits continue to apply.
The EF67 board has no starter-kit VBUS-output GPIO. Use a powered MHL
adapter. The driver's class connection_state attribute is read-only.

Build success does not verify video or audio output. Without an adapter,
probe can verify the SPI device ID/revision and ordinary USB/charging can
be checked. An adapter and display are required to verify EDID, HDMI
video/audio, HDCP, remote control, charging and USB restoration on unplug.
