EF67S camera power support
=========================

Target: IM-A930S, stock firmware S0226114.

This restores the stock camera GPIO mappings and the EEPROM power-sequence
name sensor_gpio_cam0_avdd_en. It does not implement sensor imaging, OIS or
camera HAL compatibility. DTS voltages, regulators, GPIOs and delays are
unchanged.

Stock GPIO sequence ABI
----------------------

msm_camera_init_gpio_pin_tbl at 0xc06e2714 allocates 46 bytes: fifteen u16
GPIO numbers followed by fifteen validity bytes and alignment padding.
msm_camera_power_up/down accept IDs 0 through 13. Existing IDs 0 through 8
are preserved; the additional slots are:

  ID  Name                         DT property      Stock GPIO
   9  SENSOR_GPIO_CAM0_SVDD0_EN    qcom,svdd0-en          126
  10  SENSOR_GPIO_CAM0_SVDD1_EN    (no DT mapping)
  11  SENSOR_GPIO_CAM0_AVDD_EN     qcom,avdd-en           125
  12  SENSOR_GPIO_CAM1_SVDD_EN     qcom,svdd1-en           80
  13  SENSOR_GPIO_CAM1_AVDD_EN     qcom,avdd1-en           83

The front qcom,svdd1-en property selects ID 12, not ID 10. Stock pin-table
property literals are at 0xc06e2d18 through 0xc06e2d9c. The restored table
also includes stock qcom,gpio-vio, qcom,gpio-vaf and qcom,gpio-af-pwdm.

The EEPROM parser at 0xc06e1dc0 compares sensor_gpio_cam0_avdd_en via the
literal at 0xc06e22ac and stores ID 11 at 0xc06e1fe0. DT GPIO configuration
0/1 is converted to GPIO_OUT_LOW/HIGH (0/2), as in stock.

EEPROM power sequence
---------------------

  1. Enable L17 cam_rvana at 2.85 V, 80 mA load; wait 1 ms.
  2. Enable LVS4 cam_rvio; wait 1 ms.
  3. Drive GPIO125 high; wait 3 ms.
  4. Drive GPIO36 high; wait 3 ms.
  5. Enable MCLK at 24 MHz on GPIO15; wait 3 ms.

LVS4 is a switch supply; its DT voltage 0 does not request a new voltage.
This code does not program or infer the external AVDD/SVDD output voltage.

After reading, disable MCLK, drive GPIO36 and GPIO125 low, then disable
LVS4 and L17, keeping the delays of the reversed steps. Explicit sensor
library shutdown sequences retain their supplied output levels.

Intentional differences from stock
---------------------------------

Stock shutdown at 0xc06e3550 through 0xc06e3564 uses gpio_num[config_val]
as the output value. In the stock EEPROM shutdown, config_val remains 2;
slot 2 is unmapped and zero, so the pins happen to go low. A requested low
(0), however, indexes slot 0 containing GPIO36 and would drive high.
Use config_val directly and explicitly set generated GPIO shutdown steps
low. The normal EEPROM shutdown levels match stock without relying on an
unmapped slot being zero.

Validate power-up indices, GPIO mappings and levels before enabling
supplies. GPIO requests are transactional via gpio_request_array; errors
stop startup before supplies are enabled. Regulator, clock, mux and CCI
failures unwind completed steps in reverse order. A mux configuration
failure releases its own initialization. Fix the CCI failure path's double
reference-count decrement and release CCI after a flash GPIO request fails.

Verification and remaining work
-------------------------------

Compared all twelve stock GPIO property literals/sequence IDs and the
EEPROM AVDD parser ID. Compared EEPROM, rear and front DT power properties,
GPIO numbers and regulator targets against the stock DT: all match.

Executed extracted C functions with hardware stubs to check parsing,
allocation failures, GPIO request rollback, regulator/clock/CCI failures,
invalid input rejection and explicit/generated shutdown order and levels.
The actual mux helper also passed initialization/configuration failure
checks. EF67 zImage/dtbs and the common camera subtree with EF67 disabled
built successfully.

No hardware boot or EEPROM read has been verified for this change. Sensor
library power sequences, cam_r* regulator ID translation, IMX214/S5K6B2YX,
OIS and userspace camera ABI compatibility still need separate work.

The VegaDevTeam msm8974 IMX214 extension also has camera power GPIO IDs,
but its header omits flash slots 7/8. Its numeric ABI cannot be copied to
EF67S; the mappings above follow the EF67S stock binary.
